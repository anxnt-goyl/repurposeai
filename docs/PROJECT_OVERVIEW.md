# Project Overview: RepurposeAI

## Mission
To transform long-form audiovisual media into high-signal, platform-adapted content systems while preserving the host's authentic voice and keeping the creator firmly in control as Editor-in-Chief.

## Executive Summary
Traditional content repurposing is an arduous, low-leverage activity consuming 4–6 hours per recorded conversation. Podcasters, YouTubers, and founders are caught on a treadmill: preparing, recording, publishing a single link, and starting from scratch each week. 

RepurposeAI treats every recorded conversation as an unmined quarry. Through intelligent diarization, contextual thesis extraction, moment ranking, and platform-native translation, it converts one 45-minute episode into 12 scored moments, 5 LinkedIn posts, 3 X threads, 3 Instagram carousels, 1 long-form article, and 1 newsletter issue in under 30 minutes.

## Core Philosophical Tenets
1. **Context-First Synthesis:** Audio is analyzed holistically before moments are scored.
2. **Platform Dialect Fidelity:** No cross-posting of identical copy. Each network receives tailored framing.
3. **Zero-Slop Discipline:** The AI proposes drafts; the human approves or rejects.
4. **Calm Editorial Aesthetic:** Software designed for deep creative focus, not flashing AI gimmicks.
