# Future Product Roadmap

The following post-MVP features are planned for subsequent production releases:

### 1. Direct Publishing & Scheduling
- Official OAuth integrations for LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Threads, and Substack.
- Visual content calendar with drag-and-drop scheduling across optimal timezone distribution slots.

### 2. Video & Multi-Modal Automation
- Automatic 9:16 short-video vertical clipping with animated kinetic captions.
- AI B-roll generation and filler-word removal.
- Thumbnail generation and branded card exports.

### 3. Voice & Personalization
- Fine-tuned brand voice models trained on host's past 50 articles and tweets.
- Multi-speaker voice profile differentiation.
- Multilingual transcription and dynamic localized translations.

### 4. Collaboration & Enterprise
- Multi-seat team workspaces for creative directors and agency copywriters.
- In-line commenting and guest approval portals.
- Spotify and Apple Podcasts RSS ingestion feeds.
- Post-publication reach and engagement analytics.
