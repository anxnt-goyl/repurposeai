# RepurposeAI Demo & Evaluation Guide

Follow this guided walkthrough to experience the complete RepurposeAI workflow:

### 1. Landing Page Exploration
- Navigate to `/`
- Review the product-first hero, real interface preview, and platform adaptation comparisons.
- Toggle between **Obsidian** and **Paper** themes using the top-right button.

### 2. Enter Command Center
- Click **Start Repurposing** or navigate to `/dashboard`.
- Notice the 4 metrics (Episodes: 24, Assets: 186, Saved: 42 hrs, Approval: 92%).
- View the active project card for "The Future of AI Agents".

### 3. Ingestion & Multi-Stage Processing
- Click **+ New Repurpose**.
- Click **Load Showcase** (or drop any audio/video file) → Click **Analyze Episode**.
- Watch the 7-stage animated pipeline (Uploading → Transcribing → Understanding → Moments → Generating → Adapting → Finalizing).

### 4. Explore Content Workspace
- **Overview Tab:** Read the summary, key takeaways, and chapter breakdown.
- **Transcript Tab:** Search terms (e.g., "Elena" or "quarry"), observe the `02:41 HIGH VALUE` badge, and click to view the moment.
- **Moments Tab:** Filter moments by `Insight`, `Hook`, or `Controversial`. Click **Generate Content** on Moment #01.

### 5. Split Editor & The WOW Moment
- In the Split Editor:
  - Left panel displays the original timestamp, verbatim quote, detected moment type, and audience insight.
  - Right panel displays the generated draft.
  - Click **Make Concise** to observe the draft dynamically regenerate into a punchier executive memo.
  - Click **Approve Draft** to trigger the confetti celebration and update the review counter.

### 6. Review Gate & Batch Export
- Switch to the **Review Tab**: Check progress (14 approved, 2 needs review, 2 rejected).
- Click **Export Final Assets**:
  - Choose between Markdown, Plain Text, or JSON.
  - Click **Download File** to generate an immediate client-side file.

### 7. Assistant Chat with Active Context
- Navigate to **Chat** in the left sidebar.
- Inspect **Recent Chats** organized by `Today`, `Yesterday`, and `Previous 7 days`.
- Select or type a query: *"Find the strongest hooks"*.
- Inspect the structured output with source timestamps and platform recommendations.

### 8. Settings & Theme Persistence
- Navigate to **Settings**.
- Click between **Obsidian** and **Paper** to verify immediate theme switching and storage persistence.
