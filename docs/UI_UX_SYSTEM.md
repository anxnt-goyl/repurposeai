# UI/UX Design System

## Design Philosophy: Calm Editorial Workspace
RepurposeAI rejects generic "AI SaaS" design tropes:
- No purple neon glows or gradient cards.
- No floating glossy 3D spheres or mascot illustrations.
- No card-inside-a-card nesting.
- No fake vanity telemetry bars.

## Typography
- **Primary Interface:** Plus Jakarta Sans (clean, high-density, legible).
- **Hero & Editorial Accent:** Instrument Serif (tasteful editorial craftsmanship).
- **Numeric & Metadata:** JetBrains Mono with tabular figures (`font-mono tabular-nums`).

## Color System: 60-30-10 Rule
- **60% Neutral Canvas:** Pure off-black (`#0b0c0e`) in Obsidian; Warm off-white (`#fbfbf9`) in Paper.
- **30% Structural Surfaces:** Charcoal (`#121418`) in Obsidian; Soft cream (`#ffffff`) in Paper.
- **10% Restrained Accent:** Warm Amber (`#f59e0b` / `#d97706`).

## The Two Themes
1. **Obsidian:** Dark workspace built for focused evening editing sessions.
2. **Paper:** Warm light editorial workspace inspired by broadsheet typography.
Both themes persist across browser sessions in `localStorage`.
